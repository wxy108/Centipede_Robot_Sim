# Exact-contact stochastic channel validation

**Date:** 7 August 2026  
**Morphologies:** \(N\in\{4,6,8,10\}\)  
**Output root:** `exp_data/reliability/step5_exact_channel_validation/`  
**Terrain:** flat  
**Yaw half-amplitude:** \(15^\circ\)  
**Marginal erasure probability:** \(q=0.10\)  
**Temporal burst length:** \(K=1\)

## 1. Scope

This stage validates the stochastic binary contact channel. It does **not**
compare locomotion performance and does not use displacement to accept or
reject either channel.

For the locked alternating spatial gait,

\[
\mathcal A_\phi=\{0,\pi\},
\qquad
G=|\mathcal A_\phi|=2,
\qquad
m=\frac{N}{2}.
\]

The resulting morphology grid is

\[
(N,m)\in\{(4,2),(6,3),(8,4),(10,5)\}.
\]

For a phase-side support event \(e\), let
\(Z_{e,j}\in\{0,1\}\) indicate erasure of homologous copy
\(j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\). Complete loss of the phase group is

\[
F_e=\prod_{j=1}^{m} Z_{e,j}.
\]

The two registered endpoints are

\[
\Pr(F_e=1)=
\begin{cases}
q^m, & Z_{e,1},\ldots,Z_{e,m}\ \text{independent},\\[3pt]
q, & Z_{e,1}=\cdots=Z_{e,m}\ \text{almost surely}.
\end{cases}
\]

## 3. High-sample mask-law audit

Each channel-morphology pair used 500,000 counter-seeded events. A law passed
when the theoretical probability lay inside the two-sided 99.9% Wilson score
interval, each copy's marginal interval contained \(q\), the dependence
structure matched the named channel, and exact replay succeeded.

All rows passed. In the independent channel the maximum absolute pairwise
correlation was 0.00221. In the fully correlated channel, every mask was an
exact broadcast and the maximum deviation from unit pairwise correlation was
\(2.22\times10^{-16}\).

The same master seed and event key were used across morphology. Independent
masks for smaller \(m\) were exact prefixes of the \(m=5\) mask, and fully
correlated draws were identical across \(N\). This common-random-number design
prevents a morphology comparison from silently changing the noise realization.

## 4. MuJoCo physical realization audit

Each channel-morphology pair was run for 20 gait periods, producing 80 sampled
phase-side group events. These short physical trials verify implementation;
their empirical group-failure fractions are not used to estimate the channel
law.

For every selected module \(i\), both physical sides were disabled throughout
the active support window:

\[
\operatorname{contype}_{i,L}
=\operatorname{conaffinity}_{i,L}
=\operatorname{contype}_{i,R}
=\operatorname{conaffinity}_{i,R}=0.
\]

The realized bilateral contact impulse was audited directly from MuJoCo
contact forces:

\[
J^\perp_{e,j,s}
=
\int_{\mathcal W_e} f^\perp_{i_{g,j},s}(t)\,\mathrm dt,
\qquad s\in\{L,R\}.
\]

Across all eight trials,

\[
\max_{e,j,s}J^\perp_{e,j,s}=0\ \mathrm{N\,s},
\qquad
\max_t\|\mathbf u(t)-\mathbf u^0(t)\|_\infty=0.
\]

Every mask-selected module-window produced exactly one collision disable and
one exact restoration. Every unselected collision channel retained its
original values. All states remained finite and MuJoCo reported no numerical
warning.

## 5. Interpretation and next decision

The result establishes that the simulator implements the intended information-
theoretic endpoints at both the symbolic mask level and the physical contact
level. It does not yet establish that locomotion reliability follows
\(q^m\), because whole-body dynamics can transform identical channel events
into morphology- and phase-dependent displacement losses.

The next preregistered stage is therefore the forced single-erasure response
map

\[
\Delta D_{e,j}=\widehat D_{e,j}-D_0(N),
\]

across module copy, phase group, trigger side, and gait cycle. Held-out
independent-versus-correlated performance trials remain sequenced after that
map.
